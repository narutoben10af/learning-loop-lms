# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, accessible, harassment-free project. Be constructive, assume good intent while addressing impact, welcome different levels of experience, protect learner privacy, and focus criticism on ideas and work rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or sexualized language, threats, doxxing, disclosure of private student or contributor information, and sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact route in the owner's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when needed to protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and recused.

This concise policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant 2.1 and may be replaced by a dedicated project contact as the maintainer team grows.
